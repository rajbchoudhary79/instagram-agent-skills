# DM policy

## Allowed

- Replying to anyone who messaged, commented, replied to a story or answered a sticker.
- Manual, one-to-one, personalised messages to people the creator knows or engages with (e.g. a collab pitch to a peer whose work they follow).
- Automated messages through Instagram's own features, the official Messaging API, or Meta-approved partners, when:
  - the person started the interaction (DM, comment keyword, story reply), and
  - the reply is inside the standard 24-hour messaging window from their last message.
  (Outside the window only specific approved message tags apply; check Meta's current docs before relying on them.)

## Not allowed (this system will not write these)

- Bulk or cold automated DMs; "DM everyone who followed this week".
- Scraped or bought lists.
- Third-party bots that log in with the creator's password.
- Fake personalisation ("Loved your recent post!" sent to hundreds).
- Pressure: fake scarcity, fake deadlines, guilt.
- Messages asking for passwords, login codes or off-platform payments.

## Follow-ups

- Maximum two. Space them 2-4 days, then about a week.
- Each adds value (a resource, an answer, news). Never "bumping this".
- The second is the last; it closes kindly and leaves the door open.
- If they reply "no" or "stop": no more messages.

## Keyword funnels

1. The post or story asks people to comment / DM a single WORD.
2. The creator's tool (Instagram's auto-reply or an approved partner) or the creator manually sends the delivery DM.
3. The delivery DM delivers first, then asks one question.
4. Replies get personal answers from the creator.

The keyword and what it delivers live in the profile (`goals.cta.keyword`, `keyword_delivers`). Never promise a delivery that does not exist.
