# Script structures

Pick one per reel. Beats are approximate; timing comes from script_timer.py.

## 1. Myth -> fix (contrarian teaching, 20-35 s)
1. HOOK: the belief, and that it is wrong for this audience.
2. Why people believe it (one line, steelman it).
3. What actually happens (the evidence: own experience if verified, or the mechanism).
4. The fix, as an action.
5. Proof or example (verified, or a clearly hypothetical one).
6. CTA.

## 2. Mistake -> cost -> fix (20-30 s)
1. HOOK: the mistake, named precisely.
2. What it costs (time, money, clients). Real numbers only.
3. Why it happens.
4. The fix in one sentence, then shown.
5. CTA (save is natural here).

## 3. Story with a turn (25-45 s)
1. HOOK: start mid-scene (time + event).
2. The situation in one line.
3. The turn: what went wrong or surprised you.
4. What you did.
5. What changed (verified outcome only).
6. The lesson for the viewer, as an instruction.
7. CTA or loop back to the first line.

## 4. Numbered list (15-30 s)
1. HOOK: "N things that <specific outcome>". N = 3 unless each item is strong.
2-4. One item per beat; each item has its own visual and on-screen number.
5. The one to start with today.
6. CTA (save).

## 5. Tutorial / screen recording (20-45 s)
1. HOOK: the result, shown first.
2. Step 1 (on-screen text = the step name).
3. Step 2.
4. Step 3.
5. The common mistake at step N.
6. CTA (keyword for the template / checklist).

## 6. POV / skit (7-20 s)
1. HOOK: "POV: <recognisable situation>" on screen; the first line of dialogue.
2. The escalation.
3. The punchline or the twist.
4. Optional: one line of advice as text.
Keep the CTA in the caption, not the video.

## 7. Before / after demo (10-25 s)
1. HOOK: the after, on screen, in the first second.
2. The before.
3. The one change.
4. The after again, with the difference called out.
5. CTA.

## Writing rules that apply to all of them

- One idea per beat. One idea per reel.
- Write for the ear: short sentences, contractions, words the creator actually says.
- Every 2-3 beats, something concrete: a number, an object, a name, a screen.
- Cut throat-clearing: "so", "basically", "in this video", "let me explain".
- The CTA asks for one thing and says what they get.
