Stop throwing out your sourdough starter after one flat day.

I used to bin mine every time it sulked. Then I started feeding it twice a day for three days before deciding, and it came back almost every time. In 2025 I taught 220 students that rule.

A sourdough starter that will not rise is usually hungry, cold, or both. Feed it, keep it somewhere warm, and give it three days. That is the whole beginner sourdough rescue, and it fits any sourdough schedule.
