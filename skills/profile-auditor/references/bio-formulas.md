# Bio and name-field patterns

150 characters. Line breaks count. Test on a phone.

## Structures

**Who + outcome + proof + action**
```
<Outcome> for <who>.
<Verified proof point>.
<Action> ↓
```

**Problem + promise + action**
```
Tired of <pain>? <Promise in plain words>.
<Action> ↓
```

**Identity + specific value + action**
```
<Role> showing <who> how to <specific result>.
New <format> every <day>.
<Action> ↓
```

## Proof options (verified only)

Years doing the work, a specific number of projects, a credential, a named
result with permission. No proof yet: omit the line; specificity in the
outcome does the job.

## Action lines

- Free <thing> ↓ (the link delivers it)
- DM me WORD for <thing>
- New here? Start with the pinned reel.

## Name field (64 chars, searchable)

- `<Name> | <search phrase>`: "Priya | Weeknight Sourdough"
- `<search phrase> · <Name>`: "Sourdough Teacher · Priya Nair"
Keep the phrase the audience types, not a title they would not search.

## Avoid

- Lists of identities ("Mom | Dreamer | Coffee"): says nothing about the visitor's gain.
- Vague outcomes ("helping you live your best life").
- Hashtags and @mentions that send visitors away (unless it is your own brand account).
- Emoji walls. One or two as bullets is plenty.
