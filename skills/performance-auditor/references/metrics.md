# Metrics

| metric | what it tells you | serves goal | misread |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| reach | unique accounts that saw it | distribution | not the same as views (replays) |
| views / plays | times it was played or shown | distribution | inflated by loops on short reels |
| multiple | views or reach / account median | relative performance | depends on the median's period |
| saves per reach | worth keeping | authority, education | carousels naturally higher |
| shares per reach | worth sending | growth, word of mouth | DMs shares not always broken out |
| comments per reach | conversation | community | keyword CTAs inflate it |
| follows per reach | converted strangers | growth | small numbers; need volume |
| profile visits | curiosity about the account | growth, offer | depends on the profile converting |
| watch ratio (avg watch / duration) | how much was watched | reel craft | >1 possible with loops |
| engagement rate | sum of interactions / reach | broad health | mixes very different actions |

## Rules for reading

- Compare like with like: format to format, similar lengths, similar posting periods.
- Medians over means.
- n under 3 per group: no conclusion.
- Reach depends on many things outside the post (time, trends, distribution tests). Patterns in the post's own features are hypotheses to test, not causes.
- Metrics the export does not include are unknown. Do not infer retention from likes, or reach from followers.
