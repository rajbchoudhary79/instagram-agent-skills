# Planning notes

## Pillars

2-4 pillars, each a topic area the audience cares about and the creator can
talk about for a year. Shares in the profile (`niche.pillars[].share`) drive
slot assignment. A personal / behind-the-scenes pillar at 15-25% builds liking;
more than that dilutes what the account is "for".

## Mix types

| type | job | typical share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| educational | saves, authority | 40-60% |
| entertaining | reach, liking | 15-30% |
| community | comments, relationships | 10-25% |
| promotional | sales | 5-15% (more in launch weeks) |

## A weekly rhythm (example for 4 posts)

- Mon: educational reel (strongest topic of the week)
- Tue: carousel (list / framework from the same pillar)
- Thu: entertaining or story reel
- Sat: community post or promotional when there is a real offer
Stories daily (story-strategist), pointing at the week's posts.

## Monthly themes

Pick one theme per month tied to the 90-day goal (e.g. "starter month" before a
Weeknight Sourdough Guide launch). Week 1-2 educate, week 3 proof and objections, week 4
offer (if real). Keep at least one community slot per week regardless.

## Status lifecycle

| status | meaning | who sets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| slot | empty planned slot | planner |
| planned | topic + working hook assigned | planner |
| drafted | script / copy exists | writing skills |
| approved | creator approved the final draft | creator only |
| scheduled | creator scheduled it in Instagram / Meta tools | creator only |
| published | live | creator only |
| skipped | dropped | anyone, with a note |

## Re-planning after an audit

Only move weight toward a format, pillar or formula when performance-auditor shows a gap of
1.3x or more with 3+ posts per group. Otherwise, run it as a labelled test:
two slots, same week, one variable changed.
