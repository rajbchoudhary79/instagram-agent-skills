# Caption patterns

## Line one, job A (the video carries the hook)

| pattern | example |
| --- | --- |
| The payoff, plainly | The feeding schedule I use instead of a strict clock. |
| The ask up front | Comment STARTER and I'll send you the checklist from this reel. |
| The context the video skipped | I tested this on my last 20 bakes. Here's the full schedule. *(only if true)* |
| The nuance | This works for wheat starters. Rye behaves differently. |

## Line one, job B (the caption is the hook)

Use a hook formula (`hook_rank.py --formulas`) and cut it before the window ends,
on a complete thought that opens a gap:
- "Nobody warns new bakers that a flat starter is just a hungry one."
- "I stopped feeding at midnight. Here's what happened to my loaves." *(only with a verified result)*

## Body shapes

**Teach**: the step, why it works, the common mistake, the ask.
**Story**: what happened, the turn, what changed, the lesson for them, the ask.
**List**: one line per item, with line breaks; numbered with plain digits.
**Community**: a genuine question, your own answer first, invitation to theirs.

Keep paragraphs to 1-3 sentences. Blank line between paragraphs.

## CTAs by goal

| goal | CTA | notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| leads | Comment WORD and I'll send you X. | Needs a real delivery (DM via official tools). Say WORD in the video. |
| saves | Save this for the next time <specific situation>. | Works when the post is reference material. |
| shares | Send this to the <specific person> who <specific situation>. | Make the person recognisable. |
| follows | Follow for <specific recurring thing>. | Only if the account delivers that thing regularly. |
| conversation | Which one are you: A or B? | Answerable in one word. |
| offer | The <offer> is in my bio. | One link, named. |

Never: "Like, comment, save and share!" (four asks), "comment YES if you agree" (bait).

## Search phrases

People search the way they talk: "why is my sourdough flat", "sourdough
starter not rising". Put the phrase in a sentence that reads normally. Hashtags
do not replace words in the caption.

## Hashtags

- Up to the cap in platform.json; specific topic labels; last line.
- Mix: one broad-but-relevant (#homebaking), one specific (#sourdoughstarter), one community tag the audience follows.
- Never generic: #fyp #viral #explore #instagood.
